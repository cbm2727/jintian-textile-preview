Company & Factory

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

We are a manufacturer. Founded in 1998 in Qingdao, we run our own production bases in Rizhao, Wendeng and Heze, with a total monthly capacity of over 100,000 units.

Can we visit or audit your factory?

Yes. Our production bases have passed third-party and customer factory audits many times. You are welcome to visit our Qingdao office and factories.

Which trade fairs do you attend?

Hong Kong Toys & Games Fair, Christmas World (Frankfurt), Kind + Jugend (Cologne) and the Canton Fair (Spring & Autumn). Visit our booth or contact us to make an appointment.

Catalog, Customization & Samples

How can I get your latest product catalog?

Simply leave your email in the box at the top of this page, or contact us by email or phone. If you are interested, we will send you our latest catalog to browse and choose from.

Do you accept OEM / ODM orders?

Yes. We offer OEM, ODM and OBM services. Our in-house design studio can develop plush toys from your drawings, photos or ideas, including custom fabrics, shapes, embroidery and labels.

Can I get samples, and how much do they cost?

Yes. Samples usually take 14 days. Shipping is freight collect.

  • Existing designs: up to 3 samples are free. For more than 3 samples, the fee is 3–5 times the unit price.
  • Custom designs: US$100–200 per sample, refundable when you place a bulk order.
Can you put our logo and packaging on the products?

Yes. We can customize logos, hang tags, sewn-in labels, gift boxes, PE bags and cartons to your requirements.

What if I have special requirements?

Please contact us. Whether it is special materials, sizes, designs, packaging or any other needs, our team will work out a solution with you.

Orders & Pricing

What is your MOQ?

500 pcs per design for existing designs, and 1,000 pcs per design for OEM orders.

How can I get a price?

Please contact us for a quotation. Prices depend on quantity, materials, size and packaging.

What is your production lead time?

Within 60–90 days after receiving your deposit, depending on order quantity and season.

Quality & Safety

Do your toys meet international safety standards?

Yes. Our toys comply with EN71 (EU) and ASTM F963 (US). Testing fees are paid by the buyer.

How do you control quality?

We inspect materials on arrival, check during production and run a final inspection before shipment. Third-party inspection appointed by the customer is welcome.

Payment & Shipping

What payment terms do you accept?

T/T: 30% deposit, with the 70% balance paid two weeks before shipment.

What are your shipping terms?

FOB Qingdao. We can arrange sea freight, air freight or express delivery, or work with your own forwarder.

How can I get a quotation quickly?

Email may@jintiantextile.com or fiona@jintiantextile.com with the product name or a photo, the quantity and your target market. We reply within 24 hours.
