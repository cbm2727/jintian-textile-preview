Qingdao Jintian Textile Co., Ltd. since 1998
Qingdao Jintian Textile Co., Ltd is located in the beautiful coastal city Qingdao, with comprehensive research and development center, a standardized showroom, and a modernized toy production base. Twenty years since its establishment, Qingdao Jintian Textile has always been committed to developing, producing, and selling plush toys, Christmas gifts and decorations, baby toys, textiles, and other related products, and received unanimous praise from customers across the world. Qingdao Jintian Textile is willing to take the development and production needs for OEM, ODM, OBM products, and we sincerely welcome customers from all over the world to negotiate corporate and benefit from mutual development.
