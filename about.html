About
Introduction
Qingdao Jintian Textile Co., Ltd is located in the beautiful coastal city Qingdao, with comprehensive research and development center, a standardized showroom, and a modernized toy production base. Twenty years since its establishment, Qingdao Jintian Textile has always been committed to developing, producing, and selling plush toys, Christmas gifts and decorations, baby toys, textiles, and other related products, and received unanimous praise from customers across the world. Qingdao Jintian Textile is willing to take the development and production needs for OEM, ODM, OBM products, and we sincerely welcome customers from all over the world to negotiate corporate and benefit from mutual development.
Design Center
Qingdao Jintian Textile Co., Ltd. has been persistently focused on the original design and brand development, with hundreds of toy design patents and copyright certificates, especially the creation of the famous toy brand CHINOCO, and over 11, 600 sets of art plates that have been sold, which can efficiently and accurately complete various design and production needs. In addition, the company has set up a toy design studio in Qingdao. The studio comprises many experienced and skilled designers and typesetters and is constantly exploring new fabrics, developing new shapes, and carrying out the design and development of original plush toys.
Production Control
The company pays special attention to quality and can meet all kinds of production needs without compromising quality and efficiency. In 2008, the company founded the subsidiary Rizhao Jintian Textile Co., Ltd. On this basis, production bases were established in cities such as Wendeng, Heze where transportation is convenient. These production bases generally have high standard modern production equipment, such as sewing machines, laser cutting machines, and cutting machines. The experienced and skilled workers who come from surrounding areas can ensure quality and are recognized by customers worldwide.
The company's production bases have passed the factory inspection from the third party and overseas customers many times and obtained the qualification certificate. The monthly production of the production base can exceed 100,000 units, which can meet the production tasks of customers with different orders while ensuring the quality and delivery time.
Sales Team
The company also has a stable and professional sales team. Each member generally obtained a bachelor's degree and had a solid foundation of importing and exporting knowledge. The sales team is experienced, hardworking, enterprising, and dedicated and has won high praise from peers and customers.
Enterprise Culture
Providing excellent product quality and exquisite service is our orientation, and devoting love and care is our development purpose. Qingdao Jintian Textile always adheres to innovation, transition, and humanized development. Regardless of the past, present, and future, the company will carry on with a grateful heart.
